FAQ

Quick answers

How do I pay?

We agree on the package in the chat first. You pay only after you've seen the free sample and you're happy with it. For websites, the final files or live site are handed over after payment.

What do you need from me?

Your shop link, the products you want descriptions for, and a few words about your style. Your own photos if we build a site.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Up to 2 rounds of edits are included in every package. Extra changes are quoted separately.

Do you work with shops outside Etsy and Instagram?

Yes. If you sell online in a small shop of any kind, message me and we'll find the right fit.
